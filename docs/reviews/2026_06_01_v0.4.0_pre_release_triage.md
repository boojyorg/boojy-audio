the tag, or leave it as the first v0.5 item. I'd lean toward pulling it into the tag since it's cheap

## 3. The v0.5 theme: **"Trust & Legibility"** (unanimous across all three reviews)

All three reviews converged without being told to. The codebase audit proposed *"Trust under a real
session,"* the UI review *"Legibility & Trust,"* and the feature-gap review explicitly **declined to
recommend a feature cycle**, folding its cheapest items into the same hardening theme. So:

> **v0.5 — "Trust & Legibility":** make the app stop betraying the user the moment a session leaves
> the happy path (a real plugin, an undo after a destructive edit, a project from another tempo/machine),
> and make the design system actually load-bearing (one palette, painters that theme + scale).

**Then v0.6 — "Sound":** the dedicated feature cycle — stock instruments + drum sequencer + starter
loop pack + effect presets + swing-to-engine. Deliberately *after* hardening: shipping instruments on
top of the silent-VST3-reload / DeleteTrack-content-loss / recorder-thread bugs would land a
beginner's first from-scratch song on the app's shakiest code. (Cost of deferring: the flagship "no
stock instruments" gap stays open one more cycle — a demo/marketing cost, not a user-harm cost.)

  "9 passed in 0.1s" can mean nothing ran. A missing `./build.sh` in CI = a green vacuous suite.
- **C95 — CI clippy is non-fatal** (`ci.yml:145`), despite CLAUDE.md claiming "CI rejects warnings."
- **Tracker accuracy sweep** (feature-gap §3 rank 1) — Duplicate/Reverse/Quantize/Search/Favorites/Tap-tempo

These three are cheap and make the rest of v0.5 measurable.
